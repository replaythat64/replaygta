Privacy Policy – ReplayGTA Uploader

Last updated: November 12, 2026

ReplayGTA Uploader ("the app") is used solely by the owner of the ReplayGTA YouTube channel to upload videos to that channel.

Data the app accesses

Through Google OAuth the app requests the youtube.upload scope (to upload videos) and the youtube.readonly scope (to confirm it is connected to the ReplayGTA channel and to check upload status).

How data is used and stored

The OAuth refresh token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer. The app does not collect, sell, share or transfer any Google user data to third parties, and does not use it for advertising. The app has no users other than the channel owner.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored data is deleted when access is revoked.

Contact
